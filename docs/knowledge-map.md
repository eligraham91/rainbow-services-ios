## Notes on Terminology

- Use **"individuals served"** not "survivors served" (not all are self-identified survivors)
- Use **"bed nights"** not "bed-nights" or "bednights"
- Use **"hotline calls"** not "hotline contacts" or "calls answered"
- Calls and individuals are different denominators — do not conflate
- Use **"legal clients"** and **"substantive legal services"** as paired figures, not separately
- The 72.7% referred-out rate uses bed requests (2,371) as denominator, not individuals
