# Voice Rules — Rainbow Services

Binding rules for all copy on this site: pages, components, meta descriptions, aria labels, error messages, and button text.

---

## Hard bans (no exceptions)

### Em dashes
**Forbidden:** `—` (em dash) in body copy, headings, or button text.

Use a period, parentheses, or rewrite the sentence.

- Bad: `You describe your situation — the advocate gathers what's needed.`
- Good: `You describe your situation. The advocate gathers what's needed.`
- Good: `You describe your situation (the advocate gathers what's needed).`

### Semicolons in body copy
**Forbidden:** `;` in body sentences.

Use a period. If two clauses are tightly related, rephrase as one sentence with "and" or "while."

- Bad: `People violate orders; police response varies.`
- Good: `People violate orders. Police response varies.`

### "Keep the lights on"
**Forbidden:** this phrase in any context.

Use: "sustain the response," "fund the work," "strengthen capacity," or rewrite entirely.

### Rescue / savior language
**Forbidden:** "we save survivors," "rescuing women," "giving hope to victims."

Rainbow services are survivor-led. Rainbow provides tools, resources, and advocacy. Survivors make decisions and act.

- Bad: `Rainbow saves families from domestic violence.`
- Good: `Rainbow supports survivors with the tools and time to rebuild safety.`

## Prohibited phrases — full list

| Forbidden | Reason | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| "keep the lights on" | Implies precarity incorrectly | "sustain the response" |
| "fill the gap" / "fills the gap" | Donor-framing; implies government failure | "strengthens the flexible response" |
| "government grants won't fund" | Negative donor-framing | "private giving covers" / just describe what it covers |
| "the 16% that matters" | Mathematically misleading; implies government work doesn't matter | Describe private giving positively |
| "life-changing" | Cliché; over-claim | Be specific |
| "at-risk" (individuals) | Stigmatizing | "survivors," "people experiencing DV" |
| "victims" (as primary label) | Disempowering in most contexts | "survivors" (in non-legal contexts) |
| em dash in body | Voice rule above | Period or parentheses |
| semicolon in body | Voice rule above | Period |
| "this is your place to heal" | Over-promise | Describe what services are available |

## Punctuation and formatting rules

- **Oxford comma always:** "shelter, legal services, and case management"
- **Numbers:** Spell out one through nine in body copy; use numerals for 10+. Exception: all stats use numerals.
- **Percent:** Use `%` not "percent" in body copy when paired with a number. Spell out "percent" only in flowing prose.
- **Dates and fiscal years:** FY2024–25 (en dash, no spaces). Calendar years: 2025.
- **Hotline:** 310-547-9343 (hyphens, not dots or spaces)
- **Middot separators:** Use `·` (or `&middot;` in HTML) not `|` or `/` in trust strips and eyebrow labels
- **Ellipsis:** Use `…` (single character) or `&hellip;` in HTML. Not three separate periods.

---

## Tone calibration

Rainbow's voice is:
- **Direct and specific.** Name the service, the outcome, the number.
- **Unhurried.** No urgency marketing language ("Act now," "Don't wait").
- **Institutionally confident.** Not pleading. Not self-promotional.
- **Trauma-informed.** Language assumes readers may be in crisis or adjacent to it.

Not:
- Inspirational-nonprofit cliché ("transforming lives," "breaking cycles," "a beacon of hope")
- Over-clinical or bureaucratic
- Marketing-speak ("best-in-class," "innovative approach")

---

## Composite quote labeling

Any quote or testimonial that is composite, anonymized, or paraphrased must be labeled:

`[ COMPOSITE QUOTES · IDENTIFYING DETAILS REMOVED ]`

Not: "ANONYMIZED TO PROTECT SAFETY" (implies the detail was removed only for safety, not that it is composite)
Not: "NAMES CHANGED" (implies only the name changed)
